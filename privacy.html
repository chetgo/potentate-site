Potentate

Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026 (from version 1.1)

In short: Potentate collects no personal data. From version 1.1 it sends anonymous usage counts, which you can turn off in Settings. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

Who we are

Potentate is a Bible verse memory app made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

What we collect

No personal data. There is no sign-in, no crash-reporting service and no advertising, and we don't run servers for the app.

From version 1.1, the app sends anonymous usage data to PostHog, an analytics service, so we can see which parts of the app are used and make it better. It records events such as opening the app, turning a card over, opening a verse's Context notes, rating a card, finishing a day's review, opening the unlock screen and completing the purchase, with the verse each one relates to. Each install gets a random ID that isn't linked to your name, email, Apple Account or any other app. We don't use your IP address to look up your location, and this data isn't used for advertising or shared with data brokers.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings → Usage data. Nothing more is sent after you turn it off. PostHog's privacy policy: posthog.com/privacy.

What stays on your device

To work, the app keeps a few things in its own storage on your iPhone:

  • your review progress (which verses you've started, when each is next due, and your streak);
  • your settings, such as appearance;
  • whether you've unlocked the full verse set.

We can't see this information. It may be included in your iPhone's backups (for example iCloud Backup), which Apple handles under its own terms. Deleting the app deletes it from your device.

Purchases

The full verse set is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether the purchase has been made, so it can unlock the verses and restore them on another device. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase itself: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Links to other sites

The app links to Apple's standard Terms of Use and to this page. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children; the anonymous usage data described above can't be tied to a person.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If the app ever starts collecting information, we'll say so here before it does.

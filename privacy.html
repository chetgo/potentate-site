Potentate

Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

In short: Potentate collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your progress stays on your device.

Who we are

Potentate is a Bible verse memory app made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

What we collect

Nothing. We don't run servers for the app, and the app doesn't send information about you or how you use it to us or to anyone else. There is no sign-in, no analytics or crash-reporting service, and no advertising.

What stays on your device

To work, the app keeps a few things in its own storage on your iPhone:

  • your review progress (which verses you've started, when each is next due, and your streak);
  • your settings, such as appearance;
  • whether you've unlocked the full verse set.

We can't see this information. It may be included in your iPhone's backups (for example iCloud Backup), which Apple handles under its own terms. Deleting the app deletes it from your device.

Purchases

The full verse set is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app only asks the App Store whether the purchase has been made, so it can unlock the verses and restore them on another device. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase itself: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Links to other sites

The app links to Apple's standard Terms of Use and to this page. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If the app ever starts collecting information, we'll say so here before it does.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@sahod.solutions

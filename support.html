How do daily reviews work?

Each day, Today shows the cards that are due. You see the reference, say the verse from memory, then reveal it and rate how well you knew it. Verses you know well come back less often; verses you struggle with come back sooner. This is called spaced repetition.

What's free, and what does the unlock include?

A set of verses is free to learn. A one-time purchase unlocks the rest of the verses, each with its context notes. There's no subscription.

I bought the unlock. How do I get it on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. You can also tap Restore Purchases on the unlock screen.

Can my family share the unlock?

No. The unlock isn't available through Family Sharing, so each Apple Account buys it separately.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Your progress is stored only on your device. It moves to a new iPhone when you restore from a backup of your old one (for example iCloud Backup or Quick Start). Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
